Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains when and how El Harmawy Business Consultancy F.Z.E refunds payments for its services.

1. Cancellation before work starts

If you cancel an engagement in writing before we have started any work, we will refund the amount you paid, less any third-party or government fees already paid on your behalf.

2. Cancellation after work has started

Because our services involve time and professional work, fees for work already performed are non-refundable. If you cancel after work has started, we will refund only the portion of the fee that relates to work not yet performed, as shown in the quotation or agreed scope.

3. Completed services

Fees for services that have been fully delivered are non-refundable. If you believe a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, please contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will review and correct it at no extra cost.

4. Non-refundable items

  • Government, free zone and authority fees.
  • Marketplace, advertising platform and other third-party fees already paid.
  • Fees for consultations or meetings that have already taken place.

5. How refunds are made

  • Refunds will be done only through the original mode of payment.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 7 working days of approval. Depending on your card issuer, it may take a further 7 to 14 working days for the amount to appear on your statement.
  • To request a refund or cancellation, email info@elharmawy.com with your invoice number and the reason for your request.
