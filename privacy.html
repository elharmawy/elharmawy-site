Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

El Harmawy Business Consultancy F.Z.E respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.elharmawy.com and through our services, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, company name, phone number and email address, including through the registration form on the Website. Form submissions are stored securely by our website hosting provider and in our company Google Workspace account, are used only to contact you about the service you asked for, and you receive an automatic confirmation email at the address you give us.
  • Business information and documents you share with us so we can deliver our services.
  • Basic technical information about visits to the Website, such as browser type and pages visited.

2. Card payments

All credit and debit card details and personally identifiable information will NOT be stored, sold, shared, rented or leased to any third parties. Card payments are processed by our payment service provider over a secure, encrypted connection.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare quotations.
  • To deliver the services you have requested and issue invoices.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations in the UAE.

4. Sharing your information

We share your information only where needed to deliver your service (for example with a government authority or service provider you asked us to deal with), or where required by law. We do not sell your information.

5. Cookies and analytics

The Website may use basic cookies to remember preferences such as your language. We also use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Website (for example, pages viewed, time on the site, and whether the quote form was started or sent). Google Analytics uses cookies and processes this data on our behalf under Google's terms; we do not use it to identify you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

6. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep it only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

7. Policy changes

The Website policies and Terms & Conditions may be changed or updated occasionally to meet requirements and standards. Customers are encouraged to visit these sections frequently to stay updated about the changes on the Website. Modifications will be effective on the day they are posted.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@elharmawy.com.
